AI for Sciences

Artificial intelligence methods for scientific discovery, engineering design and high-fidelity simulation.

Latest Progress

Current research progress

Updated 27 August 2026

01

Scientific learning framework

A programme framework is being defined for combining foundation models, reinforcement learning and physics-aware computation.

02

Simulation benchmarks

Benchmark requirements are being scoped for CAE, collision and mechanics simulation, with emphasis on accuracy, robustness and reproducibility.

03

Design-to-simulation workflow

A common workflow is under development to connect AI-assisted CAD design with downstream analysis and optimisation.
